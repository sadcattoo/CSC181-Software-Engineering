## 1. User group

**Who are you designing for?**  
People who want to enjoy the Diyandi Festival, including residents, tourists, and visitors.

**Why might this group need support during Diyandi?**  
They need reliable updates on the whereabouts and schedules of every event, especially during a busy festival when plans can change.

---

## 2. Situation or need

**What is this group trying to do during Diyandi?**  
They want to check the tally of competitions, locate every event, and enjoy the festival without missing important activities.

---

## 3. Problem or inconvenience

**What may make this task difficult, confusing, unsafe, slow, or inconvenient?**  
Event information is often scattered across posters, social media, and word of mouth, so visitors may not know where or when an event happens. Older adults and first-time visitors may find this especially confusing, and they may miss events or waste time searching in large crowds.

---

## 4. Proposed digital solution

**What digital tool would you propose?**  
I propose a mobile app and website focused only on Diyandi events and festivities, with a simple layout and large, readable text.

**How would it help the intended users?**  
Users can pinpoint each activity on a map and check the schedule in one place. The system would also show results of past events, so users who missed a show can still catch up.
---

## 5. Things users can do

Describe **two specific actions** that users could perform using your proposed system.

1. See the list of events for each day, including times and any schedule changes.
2. Locate the exact venue of an event (e.g., the Ms. Diyandi pageant) on a map.

---

## 6. Important qualities

Identify **two qualities** that would make your proposed system useful. You may consider whether it should be easy to use, fast, reliable, safe, private, accessible, multilingual, low-data, clear, or available during high demand.

### Quality 1: Accessible

**Why does this matter to users?**  
Users come from different age groups, so the system must be easy to navigate and use. Large text, clear buttons, and support for both Filipino and English would help older adults and visitors feel included.

### Quality 2: Convenient

**Why does this matter to users?**  
The system should stay fast and usable during high user traffic. No one wants to use a system that takes ages to load information while standing in a crowd.

---

## 7. How to tell whether the solution helped

How could you determine whether your proposed solution actually helped users?

I would collect feedback and usability ratings from users after the festival. I would also observe whether users can find an event and its location quickly, and whether fewer people report getting lost or missing events.
